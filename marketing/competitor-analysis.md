> not positioning to copy). Living doc. Last updated: 2026-09-10.

## Studied
- **The Yoga Barn** — Ubud, Bali · https://theyogabarn.com/
- **Vikasa** — Koh Samui, Thailand · https://vikasa.com/
- **Respace Prime Wellness & SPA** — central **Moscow** · https://wellness-respace.ru/

The first two are **destination yoga businesses** (staff, rooms, café, spa) — Anjoy is a curated
retreat *organizer* with a center still a vision (Lori/Gavar), so learn selectively.

## Respace Prime — the Moscow premium wellness club
*Studied 2026-09-10. Not a retreat organizer: an urban destination selling **access** and
**treatments**, 18+, "камерное пространство" / luxurious comfort away from the city.*

### Their scope
- **Facilities:** 15 m pool w/ hydro+aero massage, hammam, Finnish sauna, Russian steam bath,
  hot/cold plunge pools, Vichy shower, "sensory shower", dry flotation, thermal complex, gym.
- **Services:** SPA programs (detox / couples / men's), massages (relax, lymphatic, stone therapy),
  master-led banya rituals, seasonal rituals, pantotherapy, hatha yoga, personal training.
- **Monetize:** single visits · club cards (1/6/12 mo) · à-la-carte treatments · SPA programs ·
  gift certificates · corporate · occasion packages · merch.
- **Nav:** Bath services · Club cards · SPA services · Wellness · Offers · Fitness · Hatha yoga ·
  Gift certificate · Massages · SPA programs · SPA for two · About · Reviews · **3D tour** ·
  Masters · Articles · Contacts.

### Price anchors — Moscow premium, Sept 2026
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| **Single yoga class** | **6,000 ₽** (member 4,000 ₽) |
| Thermal/wellness access, 3 h / 6 h | 14,000 ₽ / 26,000 ₽ |
| Relax massage, 60 / 90 min | 11,500 ₽ / 20,000 ₽ |
| SPA program "Детокс", 120 min | 27,000 ₽ |
| SPA program "Афродита", 150 min | 41,000 ₽ |
| Couples program, 180 min | 49,000 ₽ |
| Private banya ritual | 19,500–55,000 ₽ |
| Gift certificates | 500 ₽ – 250,000 ₽ |

**Read it as:** a *drop-in yoga class* in this segment is **6,000 ₽**, and a 3-hour visit with no
teacher at all is 14,000 ₽. That is what this audience already pays locally — direct evidence for
revenue-strategy's "raise prices" lever and a sanity anchor for retreat day-rates.

### Mechanics worth stealing
1. **Two-tier price printed on every page** — 6,000 ₽ standard / 4,000 ₽ member, side by side.
   The membership visibly pays for itself everywhere. Cheapest possible membership sell.
2. **Club card as a bundle, not a discount** — 12 mo includes 4 guest visits, 30 freeze days, a
   60-min massage, a **20,000 ₽ gift certificate**, a PT session, a yoga class, 10% merch. That
   included certificate is quietly a **customer-acquisition device**: the member gives it away,
   the recipient becomes a new customer.
3. **Scarcity on the membership** — "лимитированное количество", price rises as stock falls.
4. **Freeze days** — kills the main objection to a long membership ("I'll travel / get sick").
5. **Gift certificates as a real e-commerce product** — any denomination 500 ₽–250,000 ₽, digital
   *or* physical, **gift packaging as a 2,200 ₽ upsell**, Moscow same-day delivery from 1,000 ₽
   with time slots, occasion copy (NY, Valentine's, birthday, anniversary, corporate).
   A checkout — not a "message us about gift cards" line.
6. **Yield management on dead inventory** — Happy Hours 15% off Mon–Thu 07:00–12:00, and 15% off
   **same-day** booking. Fills capacity that would otherwise expire worthless.
7. **Occasion packages** — bachelorette/bachelor party + 8,888 bonus points. Sells against a *date
   already on someone's calendar*, not a mood.
8. **Bonus-points currency** — a loyalty ledger that rewards return *and* works as a promo/gift lever.
9. **Bundle access with treatment** — "massage + 1–2 h thermal zone free": anchor on the paid item,
   give away the one with near-zero marginal cost.
10. **Trust stack** — awards (2ГИС 2026, SPA-премия 2026), named masters with photos, reviews page,
    **3D tour**, and branded-ingredient credibility (Comfort Zone, Thalasso Bretagne cosmetics).

### The strategic insight ⭐
Respace sells premium wellness in Moscow and **treats yoga as an amenity** — one instructor
(Mikhail), one style, no published schedule, no teacher depth. That is true across the category.
Which points at a **low-capex Moscow presence for Anjoy**:

> **Anjoy as the yoga layer inside premium venues it doesn't own.**
> Ani (and Yulia/Katya) lead regular classes, workshops or one-day urban retreats hosted at
> premium Moscow clubs, hotels and spas. They already own the room, the audience and the 6,000 ₽
> price point; Anjoy brings the competency they don't have. Revenue between retreats, a local
> pipeline that feeds retreat bookings, and **zero rent** — the mirror image of the
> retreat-hosting model planned for Lori/Gavar (there Anjoy owns the space and hosts other
> teachers; here Anjoy is the teacher hosted in someone else's space).

## Prioritized "worth adding" (tuned to Anjoy's stage)

**🟢 Now (feasible, high ROI)**
1. **Online booking + deposit** — #1 platform gap; turns inquiry → committed (e.g., YooKassa deposit link).
2. **Loyalty / referral** — capture the **94% return rate**; "bring-a-friend" / returning-guest perk. Near-free money.
3. **Richer retreat detail** — what's **included/excluded**, accommodation, meals, **cancellation policy**.
4. **Real dates + "spots left" + countdown** on retreats (urgency; both use it).

**🟡 Strategic — design into the Lori/Gavar center**
5. **Retreat-hosting model** ⭐ — let *other* teachers run retreats at the center for a commission.
   Fills the calendar without owning all content; de-risks the center financially. The "Partner with us"
   seed already exists on `/retreat-center`. **Most valuable idea from both sites.**
6. Accommodation w/ room tiers + all-inclusive pricing (when built).
7. Spa / café / amenities as à-la-carte lines.

**🔵 Later (authority + recurring revenue, needs Ani onboard)**
8. **YTT** — biggest revenue + authority line for both; Ani-led, high-margin.
9. **Online academy** — a few recorded courses → recurring revenue + global reach.
10. **Programs-by-intention** framing (Reset / Deepen / Begin) — cheap positioning tweak.

### Respace-derived backlog (Moscow / near-term)

**🟢 Now**
- **R1. Gift certificate as a real product with checkout** — upgrades revenue-strategy #4 from
  "sell gift cards" to a shippable spec: preset + free-choice denominations, instant digital
  delivery or a printed card, occasion copy (NY, 8 March, birthday, anniversary), corporate bulk.
  The `/offers` generator + `/card` already render branded artwork — a certificate is that same
  pipeline, and it shares a payment rail with the booking-deposit work (🟢 #1).
- **R2. Two-tier member pricing display** — the moment any membership exists, print both prices together.
- **R3. Occasion packages** — birthday / anniversary / bride-tribe retreat weekend. A named
  occasion supplies both a date and a buyer; the corporate page already shows the B2B appetite.
- **R4. Loyalty as a currency, not just a referral discount** — points earned per retreat/class,
  spendable on the next one *or* giftable. Captures the 94% return rate (🟢 #2) and doubles as a gifting lever.
- **R5. Trust-stack parity** — awards/badges where genuinely earned, reviews surfaced as their own
  page, and a **virtual tour**. The Lori/Gavar animated vision scenes are already 80% of a "3D tour"
  for a center that doesn't exist yet — a real advantage, not a gap.

**🟡 Strategic**
- **R6. "Anjoy Circle" membership** ⭐ — the club card reworked for a business with no building:
  member retreat pricing, **priority/early booking on sold-out dates**, N guest passes, a retreat
  credit, online-library access, freeze/transfer rules. Directly monetizes the supply constraint —
  when things sell out, people pay for *priority* — and turns the 94% return rate into recurring revenue.
- **R7. Host Anjoy classes/workshops inside premium Moscow venues** ⭐ — see the strategic insight
  above. Low capex, local revenue between retreats, feeds the retreat funnel. Pitch list starts
  with Respace and its peers.
- **R8. Off-peak / last-minute yield pricing** — only after the demand-vs-supply question in
  revenue-strategy is settled, and only on *unsold* seats near a date or on midweek/online classes.
  Never on the flagship retreat's list price.

**🏗️ The facility stack → the retreat center.** Pool, banya, hammam, massage rooms,
flotation: capex Respace has and Anjoy doesn't *today* — but Lori/Gavar is being designed from
scratch, where capex decided at design time is cheap and retrofitting wet rooms is brutal. Read as
a **build spec** it becomes one of the few revenue lines that earns with no retreat and no teacher
running — the hedge against empty weeks that pairs with 🟡 #5. Worked through in
**[retreat-center-program.md](retreat-center-program.md)** (items C1–C9).

**Cross-cutting:** list on **aggregators** (BookYogaRetreats, Retreat Guru, TripAdvisor) — discovery for the
"yoga retreat abroad" searcher Anjoy can't reach organically yet.

## Honest framing
Yoga Barn & Vikasa are destinations with staff/rooms/cafés; Anjoy is a curated organizer with a center on the
horizon. Near-term wins = **booking+deposit, loyalty/referral, richer retreat detail**. The strategic one =
**bake the retreat-hosting model into the Lori/Gavar plan.** Everything else (YTT, academy) waits on Ani + the center.

Respace changes none of that, but it adds a **third thing Anjoy can do now, in Moscow, without a
building**: sell the gift certificate and the membership as products (R1/R6), and put Anjoy's
teachers inside premium venues that already have the room and the audience (R7).
